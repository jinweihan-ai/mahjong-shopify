# American Mahjong Rules: At-a-Glance Cheat Sheet

*Averill Mahjong · averillmahjong.com/blogs/news/american-mahjong-rules · Full rules, examples and FAQ online*

## The Table

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Players** | 4 (East, South, West, North). East is the dealer. |
| **Tiles in play** | 152 under National Mah Jongg League rules. Boxed sets usually hold 160: two spare jokers and six blank spares. |
| **Hand size** | 13 tiles. The dealer starts with 14. |
| **Goal** | 14 tiles that exactly match one hand on the current year's card. Say "mah jongg" and expose. |

## Tile Count (152)

| Group | Count | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bams, Craks, Dots (1 to 9) | 108 | Four of each numbered tile, three suits |
| Winds (N, E, W, S) | 16 | Four of each |
| Dragons | 12 | Red with Craks, Green with Bams, White ("Soap") with Dots; Soap also serves as zero |
| Flowers | 8 | Interchangeable with each other |
| Jokers | 8 | Two extra jokers in a 160-tile box are spares |

## Setup and Deal

1. Wash the tiles face down. Each player builds a wall of 19 stacks, two tiles high.
2. East rolls two dice. Count that many stacks from the right end of East's wall; break the wall there.
3. Deal four tiles at a time, three rounds each (12 tiles). East then takes the first and third tiles from the top row; the others take one each.
4. Result: East holds 14, everyone else 13. East opens by discarding a tile and naming it aloud.

## The Charleston

| Pass | Direction | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First Charleston (mandatory) | 3 right, 3 across, 3 left | The last pass may be blind: forward 1 to 3 unseen tiles you just received |
| Second Charleston (optional) | 3 left, 3 across, 3 right | Any player may stop it before it starts |
| Courtesy pass | 0 to 3 tiles with the player across | By agreement |

Jokers are never passed. Every regular pass is exactly three tiles.

## Turns and Claims

- On your turn: draw from the wall or claim the most recent discard, then discard one tile and name it.
- Claim a discard only to expose a complete pung (3), kong (4), quint (5) or sextet (6) from your card, or to declare mah jongg.
- Never claim for a single or a pair, except the tile that completes your mah jongg.
- A discard is live until the next player draws and racks. Mah jongg beats an exposure call on the same tile.

## Jokers

- Stand in for any tile inside a pung, kong, quint or sextet.
- Never in a single or a pair. No jokers in Singles and Pairs hands, NEWS or the year.
- Joker swap: holding the natural tile a joker represents in any exposure, you may exchange for the joker on your turn.
- A discarded joker is dead. Winning with zero jokers doubles the hand.

## Payments

| Situation | Who pays what |
| --- | --- |
| Win on a claimed discard | Discarder pays double the card value; the other two pay face value |
| Self-drawn win from the wall | All three pay double |
| Jokerless win | Doubles again, on top of the above |
| Wall runs out | Wall game: no winner, no payments, redeal |

## Dead Hands

Wrong tile count, a false mah jongg with tiles exposed, or an exposure that fits no hand on the card. A dead player keeps tiles racked; play continues around them.

*Verify every win against the current year's card before racks are broken. House traditions on payments vary; agree before the first hand.*
