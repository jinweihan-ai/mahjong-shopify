Designed for easy, beautiful play

Each tile measures 0.87 × 0.6 × 1.25 inches — a comfortable size that's easy to read, rack, and shuffle. The artwork is engraved rather than printed, so the garden faces and Piazza Lavender backs stay crisp through years of game nights, and the high-contrast markings keep every suit clear at a glance, even for first-time players.

From first lesson to weekly tradition

New to American mahjong? The included manual and reference cards are written to get a table of beginners playing the same evening. Already play weekly? Charleston Garden No. 8 makes the weekly game feel like an occasion.

Care

Wipe tiles with a soft, dry or lightly damp cloth and store them in the carrying bag between games. Avoid harsh cleaners and prolonged direct sunlight to keep the engraving and color vivid.
